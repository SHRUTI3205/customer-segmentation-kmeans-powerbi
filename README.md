# customer-segmentation-kmeans-powerbi
customer-segmentation-kmeans-powerbi

Customer Segmentation using K-Means
About the Project

I worked on this project to understand how customer data can be divided into different groups based on their income and spending behaviour.

For this, I used K-Means Clustering and created a Power BI dashboard to make the results easier to understand.

The main idea was to find different types of customers and see how their age, income and spending score differ from each other.

Dataset

I used the Mall Customers dataset, which contains information about 200 customers.

The dataset includes:

Customer ID
Gender
Age
Annual Income
Spending Score
What I Did

I started by importing the dataset into Power BI and preparing the data.

After that, I used K-Means clustering to divide the customers into 5 different groups based mainly on their income and spending score.

I then created different visuals in Power BI to understand these groups and compare them with each other.

Customer Segments

The five groups identified in the analysis were:

Average Customers – Customers with moderate income and spending.
High Income, Low Spenders – Customers with higher income but relatively low spending.
High-Value Customers – Customers with high income as well as high spending.
Low-Value Customers – Customers with lower income and lower spending.
Young High Spenders – Younger customers who have a high spending score.

Key insights from the analysis
Some Insights I Found

A few interesting things I noticed from the analysis:

High-value customers have both high income and high spending scores.
Some customers have high incomes but don't spend much.
Young high spenders have comparatively lower income but high spending scores.
Low-value customers have both low income and low spending scores.
Average customers fall somewhere in the middle in terms of income and spending.
